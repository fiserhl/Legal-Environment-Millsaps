The Estate of Dub Pickens

An interactive exercise in gifts, wills, and probate

You are a new attorney in Yazoo City. Your first client is Walter "Dub" Pickens, 78, a retired hardware store owner with a bad heart, a big family, and a habit of giving things away.

You will interview him, draft and sign his will, follow him through seven months of gifts (and attempted gifts), and then handle his estate after he dies. Every student gets a different set of facts, so your case is your own.

How This Works

1Client Intake: interview Dub (played by the AI). He only tells you what you ask.
2Draft & Sign: build the will in the Drafting Desk, get it approved, and hold the signing ceremony.
3Life Happens: analyze each gift Dub makes or tries to make: intent, delivery, acceptance, and what it does to the will.
4Probate: Dub dies. Represent the executor and solve the problems that surface.
5Distribution: decide who ends up with what, then download your memorandum, feedback report, and transcript for CourseConnect.
Use one device and one browser for the whole exercise. Your progress saves automatically on this device, so you can take breaks. Switching devices or browsers, or using private/incognito mode, means starting over. Plan on 75 to 90 minutes total.
Privacy: While you work, your conversation is saved only in this browser and is sent to the AI tutor to generate replies. When you click Finalize, a certified copy of your transcript, will, and memorandum is stored securely so your instructor can verify it (kept about six months, viewable only with the instructor's key). Your name appears on the will you prepare, your documents, and the certified copy.
Your own words: The AI tutor is the only AI you may use in this exercise. Pasting is turned off, and for each answer the app records how long it took to write, how many edits you made, and whether you left the page. That record is printed on your certified transcript.
