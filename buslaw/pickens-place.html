The Pickens Place

Part two of the Dub Pickens story: an interactive exercise in real property

Dub is gone, his estate has closed, and his house at 412 Grand Avenue now belongs to his family. They are selling it, and you are still their lawyer.

You will walk the house, sort out what comes with it, untangle the title and the neighbors, get it under contract, draft the deed, and close. If you completed the Dub Pickens exercise in this same browser, your case picks up from the will you drafted. Every student gets a different set of facts. Plan on 45 to 55 minutes.

How This Works

1Walk-Through: what comes with the house: fixtures, crops, and airspace.
2Title & Neighbors: estates in land, the tenant, an easement, and a fence that may not be where it should be.
3The Sale: the offer, the seller's duties, and the title commitment.
4Deed & Closing: draft the deed in the Drafting Desk, get it approved, and close.
5Wrap-Up: the closing statement, then download your memorandum and certified transcript for CourseConnect.
Use one device and one browser for the whole exercise. Your progress saves automatically on this device, so you can take breaks. Switching devices or browsers, or using private/incognito mode, means starting over. Plan on 75 to 90 minutes total.
Privacy: While you work, your conversation is saved only in this browser and is sent to the AI tutor to generate replies. When you click Finalize, a certified copy of your transcript, deed, and memorandum is stored securely so your instructor can verify it (kept about six months, viewable only with the instructor's key). Your name appears on the deed you prepare, your documents, and the certified copy.
Your own words: The AI tutor is the only AI you may use in this exercise. Pasting is turned off, and for each answer the app records how long it took to write, how many edits you made, and whether you left the page. That record is printed on your certified transcript.
